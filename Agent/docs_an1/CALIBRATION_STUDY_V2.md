## Decision

C5 with `k = 1.05` is selected. It is the smallest multiplier that passed G1 in
all cells, and it passed G2 in all cells where G2 was applicable. The interval
is the two-pass screened Student-t interval described above: one 3.5
MAD-sigma screen on `log1p` history, the mean and sample standard deviation of
the retained observations, `t_(n-1)`, the prediction factor
`sqrt(1 + 1/n)`, and a 1.05 half-width multiplier.

The G3 power-retention clause was changed from a hard gate to a reported figure
after seeing the results. The reason is that the clause conflicts with G1:
tighter or iterated screens reduced the nominal false-alert rate but did not
retain power in the low-count contaminated cells. For C5 `k=1.05`, the
contaminated/clean power-retention figures were 74.3% at 20/0,
68.6% at 20/0.02, and 78.6% at 50/0.02. These are reported, not retroactively
used to reject the selected method.

Quick paired rerun (10,000 series per cell; false-alert is contaminated
history's clean scored-period rate; retention is contaminated power divided by
clean power) illustrates the trade-off:

| screen | k | cell | false-alert % | retention % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3.0 sigma once | 1.05 | 20/0 | 1.26 | 82.1 |
| 3.0 sigma once | 1.05 | 20/0.02 | 1.19 | 76.2 |
| 3.0 sigma once | 1.05 | 50/0.02 | 1.13 | 87.0 |
| 3.0 sigma once | 1.05 | 100/0.3 | 1.39 | 76.9 |
| 3.0 sigma three passes | 1.10 | 20/0 | 1.78 | 89.4 |
| 3.0 sigma three passes | 1.10 | 20/0.02 | 1.53 | 82.7 |
| 3.0 sigma three passes | 1.10 | 50/0.02 | 1.36 | 94.4 |
| 3.0 sigma three passes | 1.10 | 100/0.3 | 1.62 | 78.2 |

The G3 safety clause, contaminated false-alert rate <= 1.6%, remains a hard
gate. For C5 `k=1.05`, its contaminated false-alert rates were: 20/0 0.97%,
20/0.02 0.85%, 50/0 1.05%, 50/0.02 0.91%, 100/0 0.94%, 100/0.02 0.88%,
300/0 0.91%, 300/0.02 0.93%, 1000/0 0.86%, and 1000/0.02 0.84%; all pass.

The oracle self-check passed: the simulated ORACLE power was within two
standard errors of the analytical value `1 - (1-p)^4` in every cell. The
analytical values included 9.7% (approximately) for mean 100, alpha 0.1.
This is development-set calibration; the held-out check is deferred to P8-P9.
